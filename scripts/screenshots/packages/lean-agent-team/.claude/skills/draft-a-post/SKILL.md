Use this when I give you a rough idea and want a short post back, in my voice,
ready to refine.

## Steps
1. Read `context/voice.md` first. The three voice words govern every
   line.
2. Find the one idea in what I gave you. If there are two, pick the stronger and
   tell me you parked the other.
3. Open with a specific observation or a bit of tension, not background. The
   first line earns the second.
4. Keep it to one idea, plainly made. Cut anything that does not serve it.
5. End on a clear point or a real question. No trail-off, no "what do you think?"
   filler unless it genuinely fits my voice.

## Output
- The draft, clean, no preamble.
- Then one line: the single change you would make if I wanted it sharper.

## Never
- No hashtags, no hype, no em dashes.
- Never invent a result, number, or quote. If you need one, leave [ADD: ...].
